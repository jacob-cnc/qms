# WI-006 Final Part Inspection

## 1. Purpose

Define the step-by-step process for performing final inspection of completed parts prior to packaging and shipment, verifying all drawing requirements are met and the part is acceptable for delivery to the customer.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all parts that have completed all machining operations and are ready for final acceptance including:
- Parts completing their last operation on the Job Traveler (QF-004)
- Parts returning from outside processing (plating, heat treat, coating)
- Reworked parts being re-inspected after corrective action
- First Article Inspection (FAI) parts require this inspection PLUS additional documentation per customer requirements

## 3. Required Tools and Equipment

- Calibrated micrometers (full range needed for part — 0-1", 1-2", 2-3", etc.)
- Calibrated digital calipers (6" and/or 12")
- Calibrated height gauge and surface plate
- Calibrated bore gauges or telescoping gauges (for ID dimensions)
- Thread gauges (go/no-go) for all threaded features
- Surface roughness comparator or profilometer (if surface finish specified)
- Optical comparator (for profile/radius verification if applicable)
- Gauge pins, radius gauges (as required by drawing)
- Dial indicators (for runout, concentricity, perpendicularity checks)
- Customer drawing (current revision — verify against Drawing Register)
- QF-006 Final Inspection Report (blank)
- Job Traveler (QF-004) with all prior operations signed off
- QF-005 In-Process Inspection Record (for reference/comparison)
- Good lighting (minimum 50 foot-candles at inspection surface)

## 4. Safety Precautions

- Handle parts carefully to avoid damage — parts at final inspection represent full machining investment.
- Wear gloves when handling parts susceptible to fingerprint corrosion (bare aluminum, polished steel).
- Allow parts from outside processing to fully off-gas before handling (some coatings/plating require 24-hour cure).
- Sharp edges may still be present on as-machined surfaces — handle with care.
- Verify all measurement instruments are calibrated before use (check sticker for due date).

## 5. Procedure

### Pre-Inspection Setup

1. Verify all operations on the Job Traveler (QF-004) are signed off as complete. If any operation is unsigned, return the part to production — do not inspect incomplete parts.
2. Retrieve the current revision customer drawing. Verify revision matches the Job Traveler and Drawing Register.
3. Clean the part thoroughly. Remove all chips, coolant residue, oil, and handling debris from all surfaces.
4. Allow the part to reach ambient temperature (minimum 15 minutes for tight-tolerance work).
5. Select a blank QF-006 Final Inspection Report. Record job number, part number, drawing revision, quantity inspected, and date.

### Dimensional Inspection

6. Identify ALL dimensions on the drawing — both critical and non-critical. Final inspection checks ALL dimensions, not just a sample.
7. Measure each dimension systematically (work through the drawing feature by feature or balloon by balloon).
8. Record each measurement on QF-006 including:
   - Characteristic/balloon number
   - Nominal value and tolerance
   - Actual measured value
   - Instrument used (asset ID)
   - Pass/Fail
9. For GD&T callouts (concentricity, runout, perpendicularity, position, profile), set up appropriate measurement method on the surface plate and record results.
10. Check all threaded features with go/no-go gauges. Go gauge must pass freely; no-go gauge must not engage more than 3 turns.

### Surface Finish Verification

11. If surface finish is specified on the drawing (Ra value or finish symbol), verify using:
    - Surface roughness comparator (visual/tactile comparison) for general requirements
    - Profilometer measurement for specific Ra callouts
12. Record surface finish results on QF-006.

### Visual Inspection

13. Inspect all surfaces under good lighting for:
    - Burrs remaining on finished edges (must be removed per drawing or shop standard)
    - Tool marks exceeding surface finish requirement
    - Scratches, dents, or handling damage
    - Discoloration or staining (especially on plated/coated parts)
    - Correct part marking (if required per WI-008)
14. Verify all features are present per drawing — no missing holes, slots, chamfers, or radii.
15. Verify no unintended features (extra holes, witness marks from fixturing that shouldn't remain).

### Accept/Reject Decision

16. Review all measurements and visual findings on QF-006.
17. Make the final decision:
    - **ALL dimensions within tolerance AND all visual criteria met:** Part ACCEPTED. Sign QF-006 as accepted. Record acceptance on Job Traveler (QF-004).
    - **ANY dimension out of tolerance OR visual defect found:** Part REJECTED. Proceed to Nonconformance Response (Section 7).

### Documentation

18. File completed QF-006 in the job folder.
19. If this is a First Article Inspection, compile the complete FAI package per customer requirements (typically AS9102 or equivalent).
20. Compare final inspection results to in-process records (QF-005) to verify consistency.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- All dimensions within drawing tolerance (100% inspection — every dimension checked)
- All GD&T requirements met
- Thread gauges pass (go/no-go)
- Surface finish within drawing specification
- No visual defects (burrs, scratches, dents, missing features)
- Part marking present and legible (if required)
- Part clean and free of contamination

## 7. Nonconformance Response

If any acceptance criterion is not met:

1. **STOP** — segregate the nonconforming part. Apply a red rejection tag.
2. Move the part to the Quarantine Area.
3. Initiate QF-011 Nonconforming Material Report per QP-008.
4. Evaluate disposition options per QP-008:
   - Rework (if material can be removed to bring into tolerance)
   - Use-As-Is (requires customer concession/waiver for dimensional deviations)
   - Scrap
5. Record the rejection on the Job Traveler (QF-004).
6. If the defect could affect other parts in the lot, quarantine the entire lot until inspected.
7. If the defect indicates a systematic process issue, notify Shop Owner for corrective action investigation per QP-009.
