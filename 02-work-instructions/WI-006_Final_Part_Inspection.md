# WI-006 Final Part Inspection

## 1. Scope and Default

Verify finished parts before packaging and shipment, including parts returned from outside processing and reworked parts. Default to full inspection: every part, every drawing dimension, note and purchase-order acceptance characteristic. Use a different extent only when specified by the customer or approved by the customer in writing, with the plan recorded in the job file. Complete a formal FAI package under QP-022 when required by the customer.

## 2. Equipment and Preparation

Use suitable in-house-calibrated instruments listed in QF-028, with status checked under QP-010, and the current drawing, traveler and applicable inspection record. The owner confirms each measurement method can verify the actual requirement before work is accepted. An equipment list or general industry-practice reference does not establish an unconfirmed capability. If the available method cannot verify a requirement, resolve it with the customer and use a qualified, customer-approved external source when appropriate before accepting or releasing work.

1. Confirm all applicable traveler operations, outside processes and inspection hold points are complete, supporting records are available and nonconformances have acceptable dispositions.
2. Verify the drawing and PO revisions. Clean and deburr the part as required, handle it without damage and allow temperature to stabilize as needed for reliable measurement.
3. Check instrument identity, condition and calibration status and record job, part, revision, quantity and date on QF-006.

## 3. Inspection

4. Work systematically through every applicable dimension, GD&T callout, thread, surface-finish requirement and note. Use methods suitable for the specified requirement and confirmed by the owner. Apply the drawing/specification criteria rather than a universal thread-engagement or finish-comparison rule.
5. Record actual results, tolerances, instrument IDs/methods and acceptance decisions on QF-006 or a referenced inspection sheet. Verify documentation-based requirements against the relevant material/process records.
6. Check part condition, cleanliness, completeness, required identification and customer-specific requirements. Compare final results with in-process records and resolve discrepancies.

## 4. Decision and Release

7. Accept only when all applicable requirements and records are satisfied. Sign QF-006 and QF-004 and attach the green **INSPECTED** hang tag under WI-008, showing QC initials, inspection date and part number. Complete final release and QF-010 Certificate of Conformance under QP-007 before shipment.
8. If any requirement is not met, attach a red **REJECTED** hang tag with the same required fields, segregate the part and follow QP-008. Hold other potentially affected product. Customer approval is required for departures from the original requirements; re-inspect after rework.
