# WI-005 First Piece Inspection

## 1. Scope and Default

Inspect the first piece after a new setup, new/revised program, material-lot change or adjustment that could affect conformity. Full inspection is the default: verify every dimension, note and acceptance characteristic produced by the operation. A different extent is used only when specified by the customer or approved by the customer in writing and recorded in the job file. First-piece inspection is required even when a formal FAI package is not required; formal FAI is controlled by QP-022.

## 2. Equipment and Preparation

Use suitable in-house-calibrated instruments listed in QF-028, with status checked under QP-010, and the current drawing, traveler and applicable inspection record. The owner confirms each measurement method can verify the actual requirement before work is accepted. An equipment list or general industry-practice reference does not establish an unconfirmed capability. If the available method cannot verify a requirement, resolve it with the customer and use a qualified, customer-approved external source when appropriate before accepting or releasing work.

1. Stop the machine before removing or measuring parts. Deburr and clean measured surfaces and allow temperature to stabilize as needed for reliable measurement.
2. Confirm drawing/PO revisions against QF-004 and REG-003 Drawing Register. Check instrument ID, condition, range and current calibration status.

## 3. Inspection and Release

3. Identify all characteristics produced at the operation, including applicable dimensions, GD&T, threads, finish and drawing notes. Plan a suitable verified method for each.
4. Measure or verify every applicable characteristic. Record nominal value/tolerance, actual result, instrument ID (for example TW-MIC-001) and acceptance decision on QF-005 or a referenced inspection sheet. Verify non-dimensional notes using applicable records or observations.
5. Confirm that all applicable requirements are met before signing QF-005 and recording first-piece approval on QF-004. Do not release the setup based on a sample of characteristics unless the documented customer requirement permits it.
6. Apply hang tags under WI-008 as appropriate to the recorded inspection stage. An intermediate inspection does not authorize shipment; final release follows QP-007.

## 4. Nonconformance

Stop further production when a requirement is not met. Attach a red **REJECTED** tag with QC initials, inspection date and part number; segregate the part and control it under QP-008. Correct the setup/process, produce and fully re-inspect a first piece before resuming. Record adjustments and affected product on the traveler and NCR as applicable.
