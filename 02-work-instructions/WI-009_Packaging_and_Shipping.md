# WI-009 Packaging and Shipping

## 1. Purpose

Define the step-by-step process for cleaning, packaging, documenting, and shipping completed parts to the customer, ensuring parts arrive undamaged and with all required documentation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all completed parts that have passed final inspection (WI-006) and are ready for delivery to the customer, including:
- Standard production shipments
- Prototype/sample shipments
- Partial shipments (when authorized by customer PO)
- Return of customer-supplied material (fixtures, samples, GFE)

## 3. Required Tools and Equipment

- Cleaning supplies: solvent wipes, compressed air (regulated), lint-free cloths
- Protective wrapping: VCI (Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor) paper, VCI poly bags, or oil coating (per material type)
- Packaging materials: bubble wrap, foam inserts, corrugated boxes, dividers
- Shipping labels and packing list forms
- QF-004 Job Traveler — Shipment Record (blank)
- QF-010 Certificate of Conformance (C of C)
- Completed QF-006 Final Inspection Report (copy for documentation package)
- Material certifications (copies to include if required by PO)
- Tape, strapping, zip ties (for securing packages)
- Weigh scale (for shipping weight)
- Printer (for labels, C of C, and packing list)

## 4. Safety Precautions

- Wear gloves when handling cleaned parts to prevent fingerprint corrosion (especially bare aluminum and polished steel).
- Use proper lifting techniques for heavy packages — get assistance for anything over 50 lbs.
- Ensure compressed air pressure does not exceed 30 PSI when used for cleaning near personnel.
- Sharp edges on parts may be exposed during packaging — handle carefully.
- Secure heavy parts within packaging so they cannot shift during transit (injury risk to carrier personnel).

## 5. Procedure

### Pre-Packaging Cleaning

1. Verify the part has passed final inspection (QF-006 signed and accepted).
2. Clean the part thoroughly:
   - Remove all cutting fluid residue, fingerprints, and shop contamination.
   - Use solvent wipe for machined surfaces.
   - Blow out all holes, cavities, and threads with regulated compressed air to remove debris.
3. Perform a final FOD (Foreign Object Debris) inspection:
   - Inspect all bores, cavities, threaded holes, and internal passages for chips, wire, tape, or other debris.
   - Verify no masking tape, layout fluid, or temporary markings remain.
4. If the customer requires a specific cleanliness standard (e.g., visually clean, solvent-cleaned, ultrasonic-cleaned), perform to that standard.

### Corrosion Protection

5. Apply corrosion protection appropriate to the material and shipping/storage duration:
   - **Steel parts (uncoated):** Wrap in VCI paper or apply thin coat of rust-preventive oil. Place in VCI poly bag.
   - **Aluminum parts:** Wrap in clean tissue or VCI paper. VCI poly bag for extended storage.
   - **Plated/coated parts:** Wrap in clean tissue or bubble wrap to protect finish. VCI not typically required.
   - **Stainless steel:** Generally does not require VCI but protect from scratches.
6. If the customer specifies a particular preservation method on the PO or drawing, follow that method exactly.

### Packaging

7. Select packaging appropriate to part size, quantity, and fragility:
   - Individual wrapping for precision parts or parts with critical surface finishes.
   - Dividers or compartments for multiple parts to prevent contact damage.
   - Foam inserts for complex geometries or thin-wall parts.
8. Place wrapped parts in a corrugated box or container. Ensure parts cannot shift during transit:
   - Fill void space with packing material.
   - Heavy parts on the bottom, lighter on top.
   - Fragile parts not placed under heavy items.
9. For large or heavy parts, use wooden crate or pallet if standard boxes cannot support the weight.
10. Seal the package. Apply tamper-evident tape if required by customer.

### Labeling

11. Apply shipping label to the package exterior with:
    - Customer name and ship-to address (per PO)
    - Part number and quantity
    - PO number
    - Shop return address
    - Package weight
12. Mark the package as required:
    - "FRAGILE" for delicate parts
    - "THIS SIDE UP" if orientation matters
    - "DO NOT STACK" if package cannot bear weight

### Documentation Package Assembly

13. Assemble the documentation package to include inside the shipping container (and/or emailed per customer preference):
    - **Packing List:** Part number, revision, quantity, PO number, Job number
    - **Certificate of Conformance (C of C):** Stating parts conform to drawing requirements, signed and dated
    - **Material Certifications:** Copies of mill certs for material traceability (include if PO requires)
    - **Inspection Report:** Copy of QF-006 Final Inspection Report (include if PO requires or if FAI)
    - **First Article Inspection Report:** If this is an FAI shipment (per customer format — typically AS9102)
    - **Special Process Certifications:** Copies of heat treat, plating, or NDT certs if applicable
14. Place one copy of the packing list inside the package. Attach one copy in a packing slip envelope on the package exterior.

### Shipping Record and Handoff

15. Complete QF-004 Job Traveler — Shipment Record with: date, customer, PO number, part number, quantity, carrier, tracking number.
16. Retain copies of all documentation (C of C, inspection reports, certs) in the job folder.
17. Arrange carrier pickup or deliver to shipping location. Obtain carrier receipt or tracking confirmation.
18. Notify the customer that the shipment is in transit (email tracking number if applicable).

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- Part is clean — no chips, fluid residue, FOD, or contamination
- Corrosion protection applied per material type or customer specification
- Packaging prevents part damage and movement during transit
- All required documentation present and accurate
- Labels correct (address, part number, PO, quantity)
- Shipment record on the job traveler (QF-004) completed

## 7. Nonconformance Response

If an issue is discovered during packaging or shipping preparation:

1. If part damage is found during handling (scratch, dent, dropped part):
   - **STOP** — do not ship.
   - Re-inspect the damaged area per WI-006 acceptance criteria.
   - If damage exceeds drawing tolerance, initiate QF-011 per QP-008.
   - If damage is cosmetic and within tolerance, document and proceed.
2. If documentation is missing or incorrect (wrong cert, missing C of C, unsigned inspection report):
   - Do not ship until documentation is corrected or obtained.
   - Contact quality function or Shop Owner to resolve.
3. If packaging is inadequate and parts are at risk of transit damage:
   - Re-package with appropriate protection before shipping.
   - If unsure about customer packaging requirements, contact customer for guidance.
4. Record any shipping issues or delays on QF-004 Job Traveler and the job communication record.
