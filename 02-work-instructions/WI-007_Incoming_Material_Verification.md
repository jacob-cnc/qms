# WI-007 Incoming Material Verification

## 1. Scope

Verify incoming raw material and blanks against the purchase order and applicable customer requirements before use. Other purchased items follow QP-004. The owner performs receiving and all other shop functions; references to purchasing or inspection describe the same owner.

## 2. Preparation

Use the purchase order, required material/process records, QF-003 and suitable owner-confirmed instruments in QF-028. Review identity, quantity, size, condition, certification and traceability requirements for the actual order. No unlisted testing or measurement capability is assumed. Resolve a requirement beyond available capability before acceptance or use.

## 3. Receiving and Verification

1. Check the supplier's customer-approved scope under QP-003 and QF-019. Match the delivery to the PO, quantity, material description and required size/condition.
2. Handle stock safely, with suitable protection from sharp edges and damage. Inspect packaging and each piece for damage, contamination, wrong form or lost identity.
3. Review required certificates against the actual material specification and order: alloy/grade, condition, heat/lot identification, chemistry/mechanical results and any customer-required origin or traceability. Do not infer material composition from appearance.
4. If required documentation or identity is unresolved, attach a yellow **HOLD** tag and segregate the material pending resolution. HOLD is a material-control status, not acceptance or permission to use. The owner obtains the missing information from the approved supplier or customer.
5. Full receiving inspection is the default: verify every received piece for the applicable acceptance characteristics, and review required lot-based records. Use a different extent only when specified or approved by the customer in writing and recorded in the job file. Verify size against the actual PO/specification tolerances rather than assumed universal mill tolerances.
6. Record results and the acceptance decision on QF-003. Keep material identity linked to its certificates and the applicable job throughout storage and processing.

## 4. Tags and Disposition

Use attached tags, without direct marker identification. A green **INSPECTED** tag identifies accepted material; a red **REJECTED** tag identifies rejected material. Each inspection-status tag carries QC initials, inspection date and part number under WI-008. For raw stock not yet assigned a part number, enter a dash for the part number and record the material/heat/lot and PO identity so it remains traceable. Carry the identity forward when material is assigned to a part/job. Yellow **HOLD** tags identify unresolved material/documentation status.

Do not use rejected or unresolved material. Segregate it and apply QP-008, including QF-011 and required customer/supplier notification. Recheck and record the disposition before changing status. Store accepted material so identity is preserved and it cannot mix with held/rejected material. Keep records under QP-001 and SUP-003.
