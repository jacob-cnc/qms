# Audit Preparation Guide

## Purpose

This guide helps you prepare for your AS9100 certification audit (Stage 1 and Stage 2) and subsequent surveillance audits. It covers what to expect, common questions, typical findings, and how to respond.

---

## Stage 1: Documentation Review

**What the auditor is looking for:**
- Complete QMS documentation addressing all AS9100 clauses
- Justified scope and exclusions (Design 8.3)
- Document control system is functional
- Quality policy and objectives are defined
- Internal audit and management review have been conducted
- Readiness to proceed to Stage 2

**How to prepare:**
- Have QM-001, all procedures, and Master Document List available
- Ensure all documents are at current revision with approval signatures
- Have your scope statement clearly defined (QM-001 Section 1)
- Be ready to explain your Design exclusion justification

## Stage 2: Implementation Verification

**What the auditor is looking for:**
- Processes operating as documented
- Records demonstrating consistent implementation (minimum 3 months)
- Effective corrective action system
- Calibration program operational
- Traceability demonstrated
- Competence evidence
- Customer satisfaction monitoring

**How to prepare:**
- Ensure all records are filed and retrievable per QP-001
- Confirm calibration is current on all gages (check stickers/certificates)
- Have recent job travelers available showing complete inspection chain
- Review your NCR/CAR log - ensure at least one complete cycle exists
- Have management review minutes (QF-015) completed

## Pre-Audit Self-Assessment Checklist

Complete this checklist 2 weeks before your scheduled audit:

- [ ] Quality Policy posted and visible in work area
- [ ] All documents at current revision, obsolete copies removed
- [ ] Calibration current on all measurement equipment (no overdue)
- [ ] Job travelers complete for recent jobs (all steps signed off)
- [ ] Material certs on file for current inventory
- [ ] At least 3 months of inspection records available
- [ ] Internal audit completed and report filed (QF-014)
- [ ] All internal audit findings closed with corrective actions
- [ ] Management review conducted and minutes completed (QF-015)
- [ ] Quality objectives data current (OTD, first-pass yield, complaints)
- [ ] Training records current (QF-016, QF-036)
- [ ] Risk register reviewed and current (QF-023)
- [ ] Supplier evaluations current for active suppliers (QF-017)
- [ ] FOD checklists current (daily, filed for past 3 months)
- [ ] NCR/CAR log up to date with closure evidence
- [ ] Customer property log current (QF-024)
- [ ] PM records current - no overdue maintenance (QF-021)

## Common Auditor Questions (with Guidance)

1. **"How do you ensure you understand customer requirements before starting work?"**
   - Reference QP-002 Contract Review, show completed QF-001 checklists

2. **"How do you control your documents and prevent use of obsolete revisions?"**
   - Reference QP-001, show Master Document List, demonstrate filing system

3. **"Show me how you maintain traceability from raw material to shipped part."**
   - Walk through a job: material cert → receiving inspection → job traveler → final inspection → C of C

4. **"How do you handle nonconforming product?"**
   - Reference QP-008, show NCR examples, segregation area, disposition process

5. **"How do you ensure your measuring equipment is accurate?"**
   - Reference QP-010, show QF-020 calibration log, certificates, recall system

6. **"Who performs your internal audits and how is independence maintained?"**
   - Explain contracted auditor arrangement per QP-011

7. **"How do you manage risk?"**
   - Show QF-023 Risk Register, reference Risk Matrix, explain review cycle

8. **"What are your quality objectives and how are you performing?"**
   - Show QM-003 and QF-037 with current data and trend direction

9. **"How do you prevent counterfeit parts?"**
   - Reference QP-016, explain sourcing controls, show supplier verification

10. **"How do you ensure competence for the work you perform?"**
    - Show QF-036 Training Matrix, QF-016 Training Records, explain self-assessment

11. **"What happens when a customer complains?"**
    - Reference QP-023, show QF-022 process, link to corrective action

12. **"How do you control customer-supplied material or drawings?"**
    - Reference QP-021, show QF-024 log, drawing register, storage method

## Top 10 Machine Shop Findings and How to Avoid Them

| # | Common Finding | Prevention |
|---|----------------|------------|
| 1 | Calibration overdue on gage(s) | Set calendar reminders per QF-030 schedule |
| 2 | Job traveler steps not signed/dated | Make it habit - sign each step as completed |
| 3 | No evidence of contract review | Complete QF-001 for every new job, even repeat orders |
| 4 | Material cert missing for stock material | Inspect and file cert at receiving, before material goes to rack |
| 5 | Obsolete drawing in use at machine | Check revision at job start per WI procedures |
| 6 | Training records incomplete | Update QF-036 quarterly, document any new skill/process |
| 7 | Risk register not reviewed/updated | Review at every management review minimum |
| 8 | No evidence of supplier evaluation | Complete QF-017 for each supplier annually |
| 9 | Corrective action not verified for effectiveness | Schedule 30-day follow-up when closing CARs |
| 10 | Management review inputs incomplete | Use QF-015 template - covers all required inputs |

## Day-of-Audit Preparation

**The day before:**
- Clean and organize the shop (demonstrates 7.1.4 work environment)
- Verify all calibration stickers are visible and current
- Print/organize recent records for easy retrieval
- Review your quality policy - be prepared to state it from memory
- Ensure computer files are accessible and organized

**Day of audit:**
- Have QM-001, procedures binder, and forms binder readily accessible
- Have 2-3 recent completed job packages ready to walk through
- Keep coffee available - auditors appreciate hospitality
- Be honest - if you do not know, say so and offer to find out
- Take notes on everything the auditor says

## Nonconformity Response Template

When you receive a nonconformity from the auditor, respond using this structure:

**Finding Reference:** [NC number from audit report]

**Restated Finding:** [Restate in your own words to confirm understanding]

**Immediate Containment:** [What you did right away to prevent further occurrence]

**Root Cause Analysis:** [Use 5-Why method. Identify the systemic cause, not just the symptom]

**Corrective Action:** [Specific change to process, procedure, form, or practice]

**Implementation Evidence:** [Revised document, new form, training record, updated procedure]

**Effectiveness Verification:** [How you will confirm the fix works. Timeframe: typically 30-90 days]

**Target Completion Date:** [Must be within CB-agreed timeframe, typically 60-90 days]
