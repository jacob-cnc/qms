# AS9100 Implementation Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides a practical roadmap for implementing the QMS from scratch. It is designed for a single-member LLC machine shop and accounts for the reality of doing implementation work alongside production.

## Phase Overview

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Est. Hours |
|-------|-------|-------|------------|
| 1 - Foundation | 1-4 | Policy, scope, context, objectives | 20-30 |
| 2 - Documentation | 5-12 | Write/adapt procedures, create forms | 60-80 |
| 3 - Implementation | 13-20 | Start using procedures, generate records | 40-60 |
| 4 - Verification | 21-28 | Internal audit, management review, corrections | 30-40 |
| 5 - Certification | 29-36 | CB selection, Stage 1, Stage 2 audit | 20-30 |

**Total estimated effort: 170-240 hours over 9 months**

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

**Actions:**
1. Complete QF-032 Context Analysis (internal/external issues, interested parties)
2. Define QMS scope and document Design exclusion (8.3) justification
3. Finalize and post Quality Policy (QM-002)
4. Set initial quality objectives (QM-003) - OTD, first-pass yield, customer complaints
5. Complete initial risk assessment (QF-023) - minimum 10 risks identified
6. Establish document control system (folder structure per QP-001)

**Dependencies:** None - this phase can start immediately.

**Output:** QM-001 drafted, QM-002 posted, QM-003 defined, filing system established.

## Phase 2: Documentation (Weeks 5-12)

**Actions:**
1. Adapt all 25 procedures (QP-001 through QP-025) to your shop
2. Write/adapt 9 work instructions (WI-001 through WI-009)
3. Print blank forms (QF-001 through QF-038)
4. Create equipment list (QF-027) and measurement equipment list (QF-028)
5. Establish calibration schedule (QF-030)
6. Create training matrix (QF-036) and self-assess current competence

**Dependencies:** Phase 1 complete (scope defined, folder structure ready).

**Output:** Complete document package ready for use. All forms printed and accessible.

## Phase 3: Implementation (Weeks 13-20)

**Actions:**
1. Begin using job travelers (QF-004) for every job
2. Complete daily FOD checklists (QF-026)
3. Perform receiving inspection (QF-003) on all incoming material
4. Perform and record in-process and final inspections
5. Start issuing C of Cs (QF-010) with every shipment
6. File material certs, begin building traceability records
7. Start calibration program - get all gages calibrated
8. Track OTD and first-pass yield weekly

**Dependencies:** Documentation complete, forms printed, calibration lab identified.

**Output:** Minimum 3 months of operating records before Stage 2 audit.

## Phase 4: Verification (Weeks 21-28)

**Actions:**
1. Conduct internal audit per QP-011 (use contracted auditor or self-audit with QF-013)
2. Close any findings with corrective actions (QF-012)
3. Conduct first management review per QP-012 (complete QF-015)
4. Review quality objectives data and adjust targets
5. Verify all procedures are being followed - fix any gaps
6. Ensure at least one complete NCR cycle has been recorded (even if self-generated)

**Dependencies:** Minimum 8 weeks of records from Phase 3.

**Output:** Internal audit report, management review minutes, corrective actions closed.

## Phase 5: Certification (Weeks 29-36)

**Actions:**
1. Select certification body (CB)
2. Submit application with QMS documentation
3. Undergo Stage 1 audit (documentation review)
4. Address any Stage 1 findings
5. Undergo Stage 2 audit (implementation verification)
6. Respond to any nonconformities within agreed timeframe

**Dependencies:** All previous phases complete, minimum 3 months records, internal audit done.

---

## Certification Body Selection

**Criteria for selecting a CB:**
- Accredited by ANAB or equivalent IAF-recognized body
- Experience auditing small/single-operator machine shops
- Auditor(s) with machining/manufacturing background
- Reasonable travel costs (local or willing to travel)
- Clear pricing (application, Stage 1, Stage 2, surveillance, recertification)
- Availability within your timeline

**Expect to pay:** $8,000-$15,000 for initial certification (small scope).

## Stage 1 Audit Expectations

The CB reviews your documentation to confirm:
- QMS scope and exclusions are justified
- All AS9100 clauses are addressed by your procedures
- Document control system is functional
- You are ready for Stage 2

**Typical duration:** 0.5-1 day (remote or on-site).

## Stage 2 Audit Expectations

The auditor verifies implementation on your shop floor:
- Interviews you about processes (you are all roles)
- Reviews records for evidence of conformity
- Observes processes in action where possible
- Checks calibration stickers, material storage, traceability
- Verifies corrective action effectiveness

**Typical duration:** 1.5-2.5 days on-site.

## Responding to Nonconformities

Template for NC response:
1. **Restate the finding** in your own words
2. **Containment** - what you did immediately to prevent recurrence
3. **Root cause** - why it happened (use 5-Why or fishbone)
4. **Corrective action** - systemic fix to prevent recurrence
5. **Implementation evidence** - what you changed, updated, trained
6. **Effectiveness verification plan** - how you will confirm it worked and when

## Unique 1-Person Shop Challenges

| Challenge | Practical Solution |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Audit independence (9.2) | Contract external auditor annually |
| Management commitment (5.1) | Document decisions in management review minutes |
| Segregation of duties | Use checklists and self-verification with time separation |
| Workload management | Schedule QMS activities as recurring calendar events |
| "3 months of records" | Start generating records immediately using Quick Start Checklist |

## Quick-Start: Generate Records Immediately

Do not wait for full documentation to be complete. Start these TODAY:
1. Use job travelers for the next job (even a handwritten version)
2. Start a calibration log entry for every gage you use
3. Save every material cert that comes in
4. Record your daily start-of-shift FOD/housekeeping check
5. Track your OTD on a simple spreadsheet

Every day of records counts toward your 3-month evidence requirement.
