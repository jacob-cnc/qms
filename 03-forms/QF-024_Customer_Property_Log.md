## What Constitutes Customer Property

Customer property includes any item provided by the customer for use in fulfilling their order:

- Customer-furnished material (raw stock, castings, forgings)
- Customer-owned tooling (fixtures, jigs, special cutters)
- Customer drawings and specifications (controlled documents)
- Customer data (CAD files, programs, technical data)
- Government-furnished equipment (GFE)
- Returnable packaging or containers
