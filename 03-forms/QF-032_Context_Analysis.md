## External Issues

| Issue Category | Specific Issue | Relevance to QMS | Actions / Considerations |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Regulatory | AS9100D / ISO 9001 compliance requirements | Certification required by customers | Maintain QMS, schedule surveillance audits |
| Regulatory | ITAR / EAR export control | Some customer parts may be controlled | Maintain awareness, verify part classifications |
| Customer | Aerospace OEM quality requirements (flowdowns) | Must meet customer-specific specs | Review each PO for flowdown requirements |
| Market | Small shop competing on quality and responsiveness | Niche positioning vs. larger shops | Maintain on-time delivery and first-pass yield |
| Technology | CNC control and CAM software updates | Affects programming and capabilities | Budget for updates, validate after changes |
| Supply Chain | Material lead times and availability | Affects delivery commitments | Maintain approved supplier list, buffer stock |

## Internal Issues

| Issue Category | Specific Issue | Relevance to QMS | Actions / Considerations |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Capabilities | Single-person operation (all roles) | Key-person risk, capacity limits | Cross-reference training matrix, document knowledge |
| Equipment | 3 machines (2 CNC, 1 manual) | Capacity constraints, breakdown risk | Preventive maintenance program essential |
| Knowledge | Machining expertise concentrated in one person | Undocumented tribal knowledge | Work instructions capture critical methods |
| Workforce | No employees - owner performs all functions | No delegation possible | Prioritize and schedule QMS tasks |
| Financial | Capital equipment investment constraints | Limits growth and redundancy | Plan major purchases, maintain equipment |
