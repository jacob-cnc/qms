## Legend

- **Approval Method:** Customer individual = explicit individual approval | Customer-accepted cert = approval by a certification type accepted by that customer; keep the requirement and verification evidence
- **Risk Classification:** Critical = sole source, special process, or safety-critical | Standard = multiple sources available
- **Status:** Approved = full use | Conditional = restrictions apply | Suspended = no new orders | Removed = no longer approved

---

**Instructions:** Maintain this list as the single source for approved suppliers. Use a supplier only within the recorded customer-approved scope. Conditional status does not waive required customer approval. Record the applicable customer and approval evidence for each scope. Review annually (minimum) or when scorecard triggers status change. Verify any customer-required certification remains current before use.
