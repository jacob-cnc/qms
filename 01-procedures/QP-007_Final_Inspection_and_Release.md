# QP-007 Final Inspection and Product Release

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for performing final inspection, generating conformance documentation, and authorizing product release to ensure only conforming product is delivered to the customer.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all finished parts and assemblies prior to shipment or delivery to the customer, including the review of all supporting documentation.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Perform final dimensional inspection. Review all job documentation for completeness. Generate Certificate of Conformance. Authorize product release for shipment. |

## 5. Definitions

- **Certificate of Conformance (C of C):** A signed document affirming that the product meets all specified requirements, including drawing, material specification, and purchase order.
- **Release Authority:** The Shop Owner's sign-off authorizing that the product is conforming and approved for delivery.
- **Documentation Package:** The complete set of records accompanying or filed with the delivered product (traveler, inspection reports, certs, test reports).

## 6. Procedure

### Pre-Inspection Verification

1. Before performing final inspection, verify:
   - All operations on the job traveler (QF-004) are signed off as complete.
   - All in-process inspection records (QF-005) are complete with no open nonconformances.
   - All outside processing (heat treat, plating, NDT) is complete with certifications received.
   - Any NCRs generated during production are closed with acceptable dispositions.

### Final Dimensional Inspection

2. Perform final inspection per WI-006 Final Part Inspection.
3. Default to full inspection: verify every drawing dimension, note and acceptance characteristic on every finished part. Use a different extent only when specified by the customer or approved by the customer in writing, with the requirement and plan recorded in the job file. Record results on QF-006 Final Inspection Report.
4. Verify surface finish, thread gages, and visual requirements as applicable.
5. Compare all measured values to drawing tolerances:
   - If all characteristics conform: proceed to documentation review.
   - If any characteristic is out of tolerance: initiate QF-011 per QP-008. Do not release the part.

### Documentation Review

6. Review the complete job documentation package for completeness:
   - Job traveler fully signed off (all operations, all hold points)
   - In-process inspection records complete
   - Material traceability documented (heat/lot number traced from receiving through final)
   - Outside processing certificates present and matching requirements
   - First Article Inspection report complete (if FAI required by contract)
   - Any NCRs closed with documented dispositions
   - Drawing revision matches the purchase order revision
7. If any documentation is incomplete, resolve before release. Do not ship with incomplete records.

### Certificate of Conformance

8. Generate QF-010 Certificate of Conformance including:
   - Customer name and purchase order number
   - Part number, drawing number, and revision
   - Quantity shipped
   - Material specification and traceability (heat/lot number)
   - Statement of conformance to all purchase order requirements
   - Special process conformance statements (where applicable)
   - Date and signature of Release Authority (Shop Owner)
9. If the customer requires additional data on the C of C (specific dimensions, hardness values, etc.), include per their purchase order requirements.

### Product Release

10. Sign and date the Release Authority block on QF-006, indicating the product is approved for shipment.
11. Package the product per applicable packaging requirements (customer-specified or standard shop practice per QP-020).
12. Include the C of C and any other customer-required documentation with the shipment.
13. File the retained documentation package (traveler, inspection records, material certs, NCR copies) in the job file for the required retention period.

### Customer Source Inspection

14. If the purchase order requires customer source inspection:
    - Notify the customer representative that the product is ready for inspection.
    - Do not ship until the customer representative has performed their inspection and provided written acceptance.
    - Record the customer inspection date and acceptance on QF-006.
