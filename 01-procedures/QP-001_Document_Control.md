# QP-001 Document Control

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the system for creating, approving, distributing, revising, and retiring controlled documents within the Quality Management System.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all QMS documents (Quality Manual, procedures, work instructions, forms) and applicable external documents (customer drawings, specifications, standards).

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Create, review, approve, distribute, and retire all controlled documents. Maintain the Master Document List. Process all Document Change Requests. |

## 4. References

- QF-025 — Document Change Request
- Master Document List (06-registers/)
- QM-001 — Quality Manual
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 7.5

## 5. Definitions

- **Controlled Document:** Any document that affects product quality or QMS compliance and is subject to revision control and approval.
- **Obsolete Document:** A superseded revision that is no longer valid for use. Retained for historical reference only, marked "OBSOLETE."
- **External Document:** A document originated outside the organization (customer drawings, industry specifications, standards) that is used within the QMS.

## 6. Procedure

### Document Numbering

1. Assign document numbers per the established convention:
   - QM-XXX: Quality Manual documents
   - QP-XXX: Quality Procedures
   - WI-XXX: Work Instructions
   - QF-XXX: Quality Forms and Templates
   - SUP-XXX: Supporting documents
   - REG-XXX: Controlled registers
2. Numbers are assigned sequentially within each prefix. Never reuse a retired number.

### Document Creation and Approval

3. Draft new documents using the standard document control header (document number, title, revision, effective date, prepared by, approved by).
4. Review the draft for technical accuracy, consistency with QM-001 and applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements, and clarity.
5. Record the owner’s approval and actual revision approval date separately from the document effective date. The current owner-designated QMS baseline effective date is 2026-08-10, with revision approval dated 2026-11-05; record future revision approval dates when approved. Set annual review dates and keep the master list synchronized.
6. Assign Revision A for initial release.

### Revision Control

7. Revisions follow alphabetical sequence: A, B, C, D, etc.
8. To request a change, complete QF-025 Document Change Request describing the proposed change and justification.
9. Review the change request for impact on other documents, processes, or product quality.
10. If approved, update the revision letter, dated approval, change history and annual review date. Retain the owner-designated effective-date convention unless the owner authorizes a change; never substitute an effective date for the actual revision approval date.
11. Update the Master Document List with the new revision, date, and location.

### Distribution

12. Distribute approved documents electronically (controlled folder on shop computer) or as printed controlled copies.
13. When issuing a new revision, replace or destroy all copies of the previous revision at each distribution point.
14. Maintain one archived copy of each superseded revision marked "OBSOLETE" in the obsolete document file.

### Obsolete Document Handling

15. Mark obsolete documents clearly with "OBSOLETE" stamp or watermark.
16. Remove obsolete documents from all active work areas and distribution points.
17. Retain one copy in the obsolete archive for reference. Destroy all other copies.

### External Document Control

18. Identify all external documents used in the QMS (customer drawings, material specs, industry standards).
19. Record customer drawings/specifications and revisions in REG-003 Drawing Register and the job file. Record other external specifications used for a job in its controlled requirements record. REG-001 lists the controlled internal QMS sources; it is not a register of every customer drawing.
20. Verify external document currency at each contract review (new job) or when notified of a revision by the issuer.

### Record Retention

Back up electronic QMS, job and CNC program records to an external hard drive weekly and whenever documents or records are added or changed. Test retrieval at least annually and record the result on REG-005 QMS Recurring Tasks. Protect originals and backups from unauthorized access and loss.

21. Retain quality records for at least ten years after the relevant last shipment, or after record completion for records not tied to a shipment. Apply any longer program, contract, purchase order, legal or SUP-003 requirement. Calibration and maintenance records are also retained for equipment life plus three years after retirement; the longest applicable period governs.
22. Store records in a secure, retrievable manner (fireproof cabinet for paper, backed-up drive for electronic).
23. Dispose of records past their retention period only after verifying no open contracts require them.
