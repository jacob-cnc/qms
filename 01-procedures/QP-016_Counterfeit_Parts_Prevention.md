# QP-016 Counterfeit Parts Prevention

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for preventing the purchase, receipt, and use of counterfeit or suspect parts and materials in production.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all externally sourced raw materials, fasteners, hardware, tooling inserts, and outside-processed items used in product realization.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Source from OEM/authorized distributors, verify material certifications at receiving, quarantine suspect items, report counterfeit discoveries, flow-down requirements to suppliers |

## 4. References

- QF-031 — Suspect Counterfeit Material Report
- QP-003 — Purchasing and Supplier Management
- QP-008 — Nonconforming Product Control

## 5. Definitions

- **Counterfeit Part:** An item whose origin, age, composition, or condition has been misrepresented, altered, or concealed to deceive the buyer.
- **Suspect Part:** An item for which there is objective evidence that it may be counterfeit but definitive determination has not yet been made.
- **Authorized Source:** OEM manufacturer, their franchised/authorized distributor, or a supplier who can provide full traceability to the OEM.

## 6. Procedure

### Sourcing Strategy

1. Purchase raw material, fasteners, and hardware ONLY from OEM manufacturers or their authorized/franchised distributors as listed on the Approved Supplier List.
2. When an authorized source is unavailable, obtain documented traceability to the OEM (original mill certificates, lot traceability, authorized reseller documentation) before accepting material.
3. Include counterfeit prevention flow-down requirements on all purchase orders to suppliers.

### Receiving Verification

4. At receiving inspection, verify:
   - Material certification matches the PO requirements (alloy, heat number, specification)
   - Physical markings on material match the certification
   - Packaging is consistent with expected OEM/distributor packaging (no signs of tampering or re-labeling)
5. For fasteners and hardware: verify markings match the specification called out; compare against known-good reference samples when available.

### Detection and Quarantine

6. If any discrepancy is identified that suggests counterfeit or suspect material:
   - Immediately quarantine the item(s) in the designated Quarantine Area (red tag).
   - Initiate QF-031 Counterfeit Parts Suspect Report.
   - Do NOT use the material for any production purpose.
7. Investigate the suspect item: contact the supplier for documentation, compare against OEM specifications, perform additional testing if warranted (hardness, dimensional, chemical).

### Reporting

8. If investigation confirms or cannot definitively rule out counterfeit:
   - Report to GIDEP within 60 days of determination.
   - Notify affected customers if suspect material was used in any delivered product.
   - Initiate corrective action per QP-009.
9. Disposition confirmed counterfeit material as SCRAP. Do not return to the supplier chain.

### Training and Awareness

10. Review counterfeit prevention awareness annually or when industry alerts (GIDEP notices) are received.
11. Maintain records of awareness reviews in training records.
