# QP-013 Training and Competence

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Maintain the owner's competence for work accepted by Torchworx LLC. Jacob Honick is the sole owner/operator and performs all management, machining, inspection, purchasing and shipping functions in the single shop. No employees or additional shop operators are represented by the role names on QMS forms.

## 2. Responsibility and Competence

The owner assesses capability before accepting work and when equipment, processes or customer requirements change. The owner's competence comes from more than ten years of operating this workshop. QF-036 records the owner's competency assessment and signoff for the functions performed; the entries represent one person, not separate staff positions.

Work outside demonstrated competence is not accepted unless the competence is gained or the work is purchased from a qualified, customer-approved source under QP-003. A competency signoff does not establish unlisted equipment or an unconfirmed manufacturing or measurement capability.

## 3. Training and Assessment

1. Identify gaps during contract review, changes, nonconformances and management review.
2. Use suitable self-study, practical experience, manufacturer guidance or external training to address identified needs.
3. Record new skills, methods, equipment and training used for customer work on QF-016, including date, scope, method and the owner's assessment/signoff.
4. Assess effectiveness through demonstrated work, inspection results, practical evaluation or other relevant evidence. Address unresolved gaps before relying on the capability for customer work.
5. Review QF-036 at least annually and when relevant requirements change. Review quality-system changes, safety, FOD, counterfeit awareness and applicable customer requirements during this review; record any resulting training on QF-016.

## 4. External Providers

External suppliers are controlled under QP-003; they are not employees or additional workers in the shop. For externally provided calibration, apply QP-010 and any customer-required provider qualifications. External assessments, when required, follow QP-011.

## 5. Knowledge and Records

Keep useful setup notes, proven programs, work instructions, lessons learned and customer requirements in the controlled job/QMS records. Retain training and competence records under QP-001 and SUP-003.
