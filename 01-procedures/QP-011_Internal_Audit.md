# QP-011 Internal Audit

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits to verify the QMS conforms to AS9100 requirements and is effectively implemented.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all processes, procedures, and areas within the QMS. The audit program covers all AS9100 clauses (4 through 10) and all shop processes within each 12-month audit cycle.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Plan the annual audit schedule, contract qualified auditor, provide access and records, review findings, implement corrective actions, track closure |
| Contracted External Auditor | Conduct the audit independently, prepare the audit report, present findings to Shop Owner |

## 4. References

- QF-013 - Internal Audit Checklist
- QF-014 - Internal Audit Report
- QP-009 - Corrective Action
- QM-001 - Quality Manual
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 9.2

## 5. Definitions

- **Internal Audit:** A systematic, independent examination of the QMS to determine whether activities and results comply with planned arrangements and whether those arrangements are implemented effectively.
- **Auditor Independence:** The auditor must not audit their own work. As a sole proprietor, this requires contracting an external qualified auditor.
- **Nonconformity (Finding):** Non-fulfillment of a requirement identified during audit.
- **Observation:** A situation that is not currently a nonconformity but could become one if not addressed, or an opportunity for improvement.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Auditor Qualification

1. The Shop Owner cannot audit their own processes. A contracted external auditor must be used.
2. Auditor qualification requirements:
   - Knowledge of AS9100 Rev D requirements
   - Formal auditor training (e.g., ISO 9001/AS9100 Lead Auditor course)
   - Independence from the processes being audited
   - Understanding of machining/manufacturing processes (preferred)
3. Maintain records of the contracted auditor's qualifications on file.

### 6.2 Annual Audit Schedule

4. At the beginning of each calendar year (or when the QMS is first established), create an annual audit schedule covering all AS9100 clauses and all shop processes.
5. Distribute audit coverage across the year. A single comprehensive audit or multiple focused audits are both acceptable.
6. Consider the following when scheduling:
   - Results of previous audits (areas with findings get higher priority)
   - Process changes since last audit
   - Customer complaints or nonconformance trends
   - Timing relative to surveillance/recertification audits

### 6.3 Audit Preparation

7. Provide the contracted auditor with:
   - The audit scope and clauses/processes to be covered
   - Current versions of relevant procedures and work instructions
   - QF-013 Internal Audit Checklist (or allow auditor to use their own equivalent)
   - Previous audit reports and open corrective actions
8. Schedule the audit date with sufficient lead time (minimum 2 weeks) to ensure records and evidence are accessible.

### 6.4 Audit Execution

9. The auditor conducts the audit against defined audit criteria using objective evidence: records, observations, and interviews (with Shop Owner).
   Audit criteria include:
   - AS9100 Rev D standard requirements (applicable clauses)
   - Documented QMS procedures and work instructions
   - Customer-specific and regulatory requirements applicable to the shop
   - Quality objectives and targets
10. The auditor documents findings categorized as:
    - Major Nonconformity: Absence or total breakdown of a system requirement
    - Minor Nonconformity: Isolated lapse that does not represent system breakdown
    - Observation/Opportunity for Improvement: Not a nonconformity but noted for consideration
11. The auditor presents findings to the Shop Owner at a closing meeting.

### 6.5 Audit Reporting

12. The auditor completes QF-014 Internal Audit Report including: audit date, scope, auditor name, findings with objective evidence, and overall conclusion.
13. The Shop Owner reviews and acknowledges the report by signature.

### 6.6 Corrective Action Follow-Up

14. For each nonconformity (major or minor), initiate a Corrective Action Request per QP-009.
15. Record the CAR number on QF-014 linked to each finding.
16. Corrective actions must be completed within 30 days (minor) or 15 days (major) of the audit report date.
17. The contracted auditor (or Shop Owner for minor items) verifies corrective action effectiveness at next audit or through documented evidence review.

### 6.7 Records and Retention

18. Retain audit schedules, checklists, reports, and corrective action records for a minimum of 5 years.
19. Review audit results at Management Review (QP-012) as a required input.
