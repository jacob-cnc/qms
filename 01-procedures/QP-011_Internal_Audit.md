# QP-011 Internal Audit — Owner QMS Self-Check

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Define the annual owner self-check of actual shop practice against QM-001, applicable supporting documents and accepted customer requirements. Torchworx LLC is not certified to AS9100 or ISO 9001 and does not claim compliance with those standards. This self-check is not independent and is not represented as an AS9100 internal audit.

## 2. Responsibility

Jacob Honick, Owner, performs the self-check and all shop functions. An external assessment is arranged only when required by an accepted customer requirement or separately selected by the owner. This procedure does not imply an engaged auditor or registrar.

## 3. Method

1. At least annually, compare actual practice and available records with the quality manual and applicable customer requirements. Also check significant changes and previous corrective actions.
2. Use QF-013 as a self-check aid and QF-014 for a report when useful. Record gaps, actions and completion evidence on QF-038; reference any supporting checklist or report.
3. Identify the actual requirement for each finding. Guidance clause numbers do not make an entire standard an adopted requirement.
4. Review contract acceptance, supplier approvals, production and inspection records, tags, calibration, records/backup protection, competence, nonconformances and improvement actions. Mark activities not yet exercised as such rather than implying completed implementation.
5. Record evidence reviewed, any gaps, risk-based action dates and the owner's signoff. Use QP-009 when a gap warrants corrective action. Customer response deadlines take precedence.
6. Verify that actions worked and review results during management review under QP-012.

## 4. Customer-Required External Assessments

If a customer requires an independent audit, agree the scope, criteria, assessor qualifications and timing before accepting the obligation. Keep the resulting evidence. An owner self-check does not satisfy an independent-audit requirement.

## 5. Records and References

Retain self-check records and associated actions under QP-001 and SUP-003, for at least ten years or the longest applicable period.

- QM-001 Quality Manual
- QF-013 Internal Audit Checklist; QF-014 Internal Audit Report; QF-038 Improvement Log
- QP-009 Corrective Action; QP-012 Management Review
- AS9100 clause 9.2, for guidance only
