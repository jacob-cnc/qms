# QP-024 Work Transfer Control

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for planning, executing, and verifying transfers of work between manufacturing locations (internal or to/from subcontractors) to ensure product quality is maintained during the transition.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all planned or unplanned transfers of production work, including:
- Transferring work to a subcontractor (outsourcing an operation previously done in-house)
- Receiving work from a subcontractor (insourcing)
- Transferring work between facilities or equipment

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Plan work transfers, validate receiving location capability, verify product after transfer, notify customer when contractually required, maintain transfer records |

## 4. References

- QP-003 — Purchasing and Supplier Management
- QP-022 — First Article Inspection
- QP-025 — Control of Changes
- QP-015 — Configuration Management
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 8.1 (Operational Planning and Control)

## 5. Definitions

- **Work Transfer:** The planned relocation of a manufacturing operation or process from one source to another (in-house to subcontractor, subcontractor to in-house, or between facilities/machines).
- **Capability Validation:** Verification that the receiving location has the equipment, tooling, personnel competence, and process controls to produce conforming product.
- **Transfer Plan:** Documentation of the transfer scope, timeline, validation steps, and acceptance criteria.

## 6. Procedure

### Planning the Transfer

1. Identify the scope of work to be transferred: specific operations, part numbers, drawing revisions, specifications, and quality requirements.
2. Document a Transfer Plan including:
   - Reason for transfer
   - Operations/processes being transferred
   - Technical data package to be provided (drawings, specs, programs, tooling lists)
   - Capability validation requirements at the receiving location
   - Timeline and milestones
   - Customer notification requirement (yes/no and basis)

### Capability Validation at Receiving Location

3. Before production begins at the receiving location, validate:
   - Equipment is capable of meeting drawing tolerances
   - Personnel are trained/competent for the operations
   - Inspection equipment is available and calibrated
   - For subcontractors: approved on the Approved Supplier List per QP-003
4. Provide complete technical data package to the receiving location (current revision drawings, specifications, special process requirements).

### Product Verification After Transfer

5. Perform First Article Inspection per QP-022 on the first production run at the receiving location.
6. Compare FAI results against prior production data (when available) to confirm equivalent capability.
7. If FAI fails, do not proceed to production quantities. Address root cause and re-validate.

### Customer Notification

8. Notify the customer before executing the transfer when:
   - The purchase order or contract requires notification of manufacturing source changes
   - The transfer involves a special process (heat treat, plating, NDT) moving to a new supplier
   - Customer-approved process plans reference the original source
9. Document customer notification and approval (when required) in the Transfer Plan.

### Records

10. Retain the Transfer Plan, capability validation evidence, and FAI results for the contract retention period.
