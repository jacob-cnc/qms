# QP-009 Corrective Action

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for investigating the root cause of nonconformances, implementing corrective actions, and verifying their effectiveness to prevent recurrence.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all corrective actions initiated from internal nonconformances, customer complaints, audit findings, supplier issues, or any systemic problem requiring root cause elimination.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Initiate CARs, perform root cause analysis, implement corrective actions, verify effectiveness, close CARs, issue Supplier CARs (SCARs) |
| Contracted Auditor | May identify findings that require corrective action during internal audits |

## 5. Definitions

- **Corrective Action:** Action taken to eliminate the root cause of a detected nonconformity or undesirable situation to prevent recurrence.
- **Root Cause:** The fundamental, underlying reason that caused a nonconformance, which if eliminated would prevent recurrence.
- **SCAR (Supplier Corrective Action Request):** A formal corrective action request issued to a supplier requiring investigation and response.
- **Effectiveness Verification:** Objective evidence that the implemented corrective action has prevented recurrence of the original problem.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 CAR Initiation

1. Initiate QF-012 Corrective Action Request when any trigger condition from QP-008 Step 15 is met, or when directed by audit findings, customer complaints, or trend analysis.
2. Assign the next sequential CAR number from the NCR/CAR Log.
3. Record: date initiated, source (NCR number, audit finding, customer complaint, trend), description of the problem, and immediate containment actions taken.

### 6.2 Root Cause Analysis

4. Perform root cause analysis using one or more of the following methods:
   - **5-Why Analysis:** Ask "why" iteratively until the fundamental cause is identified (minimum 3 levels, typically 5).
   - **Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagram:** Categorize potential causes by Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Environment.
5. Document the root cause analysis on QF-012. Record the method used and the conclusion.
6. Verify the identified root cause logically explains how the nonconformance occurred and why existing controls did not prevent it.

### 6.3 Corrective Action Planning

7. Define the corrective action(s) that will eliminate the identified root cause. Actions may include:
   - Process or procedure changes
   - Tooling or fixture modifications
   - Additional inspection points
   - Training or retraining
   - Supplier changes or added controls
8. Record planned actions, responsible person (Shop Owner), and target completion date on QF-012.
9. Target completion: 30 calendar days from CAR initiation unless complexity requires extension (document justification).

### 6.4 Implementation

10. Implement the planned corrective actions. Update procedures, work instructions, or forms as required.
11. Record the actual implementation date and description of what was done on QF-012.

### 6.4a Similar Process/Product Review

11a. Per AS9100D 10.2.1, determine if similar nonconformities exist, or could potentially occur, in other processes, products, or operations.
11b. If the identified root cause could produce failures in similar processes (e.g., same tooling approach on different part numbers, same material from same supplier), extend the corrective action scope or initiate additional CARs for those areas.
11c. Document the similar-process review and its conclusions on QF-012.

### 6.5 Effectiveness Verification

12. After implementation, monitor for recurrence over a defined period (minimum 3 subsequent jobs or 60 days, whichever comes first).
13. Review production records, inspection results, or other objective evidence to confirm the problem has not recurred.
14. Record effectiveness verification results and date on QF-012.
15. If the corrective action is NOT effective (problem recurs), reopen the CAR and return to Step 4 for further root cause analysis.

### 6.6 Systemic Review

16. At each Management Review (QP-012), review CAR trends for:
    - Repeat root causes indicating systemic issues
    - Overdue CARs requiring escalation
    - Opportunities for preventive action across similar processes
17. Update the NCR/CAR Log with closure information.

### 6.7 Supplier Corrective Action (SCAR)

18. When a nonconformance is attributable to a supplier, issue QF-035 Supplier Corrective Action Request.
19. Require the supplier to respond with root cause analysis and corrective action plan within 15 business days.
20. Evaluate the supplier response for adequacy. If inadequate, request revision or escalate per QP-003 supplier management provisions.
21. Verify supplier corrective action effectiveness on subsequent deliveries.
