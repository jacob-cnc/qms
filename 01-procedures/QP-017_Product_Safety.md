# QP-017 Product Safety

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for identifying safety-critical items, managing associated risks, and ensuring timely notification and escalation when safety-related nonconformances or concerns arise.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all products manufactured or processed that have been identified as safety-critical by the customer, drawing, or specification.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Identify safety-critical items from customer data, ensure awareness at point of manufacture, escalate and notify customers immediately upon discovery of safety-related nonconformances, maintain safety records |

## 4. References

- QP-008 — Nonconforming Product Control

## 5. Definitions

- **Safety-Critical Item:** Any part, assembly, or characteristic whose failure could cause loss of life, serious injury, or loss of a major system (as designated by the customer or regulatory authority).
- **Key Characteristic:** A feature whose variation has a significant effect on product fit, performance, or safety.
- **Immediate Notification:** Within 24 hours of discovery.

## 6. Procedure

### Identification of Safety-Critical Items

1. At contract review/order entry, review customer drawings, purchase orders, and specifications for safety-critical designations (S-critical notes, inverted delta symbols, "Safety" callouts, or customer-specific notation).
2. Flag identified safety-critical items on the Job Traveler and Work Order. Note the specific characteristic(s) designated as safety-critical.
3. The owner reviews safety-critical status before accepting and starting the job and communicates applicable requirements to the customer and customer-approved external suppliers. No additional shop personnel are implied.

### Awareness and Controls

4. Ensure operators are aware of which characteristics are safety-critical on their current job (verbal briefing at job start, noted on traveler).
5. Apply additional verification to safety-critical characteristics: 100% inspection of those dimensions/features (no sampling).
6. Record inspection results for safety-critical characteristics with individual measurement values (not pass/fail only).

### Nonconformance Escalation

7. If a nonconformance is discovered on a safety-critical characteristic:
   - Immediately STOP production on the affected operation.
   - Quarantine ALL parts from the affected lot (conforming and nonconforming).
   - Initiate QF-011 with "SAFETY-CRITICAL" clearly noted.
   - Perform a look-back: determine if any previously produced parts from the same setup could be affected.
8. Notify the customer within 24 hours of discovery. Record notification details on QF-011.
9. Do NOT disposition safety-critical nonconformances as "Use-As-Is" without WRITTEN customer authorization.

### Reporting Obligations

10. If safety-critical nonconforming product has already been shipped, immediately notify the customer per QP-023.
11. Retain all records related to safety-critical nonconformances under QP-001 and SUP-003 for at least ten years after the relevant last shipment, the life of the part, or the customer/contract/legal requirement, whichever is longest.
