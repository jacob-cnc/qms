# QP-004 Receiving Inspection

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for inspecting and verifying incoming material, parts, and outside-processed items to ensure conformance to purchase order requirements before release to stock or production.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all incoming raw material, hardware, outside-processed parts (heat treat, plating, NDT), and subcontracted machining received at the shop.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Perform receiving inspection on all incoming material. Accept or reject material based on verification results. Maintain receiving inspection records. Quarantine nonconforming material. |

## 5. Definitions

- **Certified Material Test Report (CMTR):** Mill-issued documentation certifying material chemical composition and mechanical properties to the applicable specification.
- **Quarantine:** Segregation of material in a designated hold area pending disposition of a nonconformance.

## 6. Procedure

### Receipt and Identification

1. Upon receipt, compare the packing slip to the purchase order (QF-002) for correct part number, material specification, quantity, and revision.
2. Verify the supplier is on the Approved Supplier List (QF-019). If not, quarantine the material and do not proceed until resolved.
3. Assign a receiving number or lot identifier for traceability. Mark the material with the identifier.

### Documentation Review

4. Verify the following documentation is present and complete per WI-007:
   - Certificate of Conformance (C of C) from the supplier
   - Certified Material Test Report (CMTR) for raw material, showing chemical and mechanical properties meet the specification
   - Process certifications for outside processing (heat treat, plating, NDT reports)
   - Traceability data (heat lot, batch number) matching the physical material markings
5. Confirm test report values meet the specification requirements (not just that a cert is present).

### Physical Verification

6. Perform visual inspection for damage, contamination, corrosion, or incorrect packaging.
7. Verify material identification markings match the CMTR and purchase order (heat number, alloy, size).
8. For dimensioned items (bar stock, plate), verify at least one critical dimension (diameter, thickness) against the purchase order specification.
9. For outside-processed parts, verify visual appearance is consistent with the process specification (color, coating uniformity, marking legibility).

### Counterfeit Detection

10. Check for indicators of suspect/counterfeit material:
    - Missing or inconsistent heat lot markings
    - Documentation anomalies (mismatched fonts, missing mill logos, photocopied certs without original traceability)
    - Material appearance inconsistent with specification (wrong color, weight, or surface finish for the alloy)
11. If counterfeit material is suspected, quarantine immediately and follow QP-016.

### Acceptance or Rejection

12. If all checks pass, record results on QF-003 Receiving Inspection Record and mark the material as accepted. Release to stock or production.
13. If any check fails:
    - Quarantine the material in the designated hold area.
    - Initiate a Nonconforming Material Report (QF-011) per QP-008.
    - Notify the supplier per QP-003.
    - Do not release material to production until disposition is complete.

### Urgent/Released-to-Production Material

14. Material shall NOT be released to production prior to completing receiving inspection unless a documented customer-authorized concession exists.
15. If material is released before inspection completion under concession, identify it with "UNINSPECTED" marking and record the concession authorization on QF-003.
