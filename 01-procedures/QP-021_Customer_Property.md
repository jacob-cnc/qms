# QP-021 Customer Property Control

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for receiving, identifying, protecting, and returning customer-furnished property (material, tooling, fixtures, drawings, or intellectual property) to prevent loss, damage, or unauthorized use.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all tangible and intangible property furnished by the customer for use in production, including raw material, castings/forgings, tooling, fixtures, gauges, drawings, data, and test specimens.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Receive and verify customer property, record in Customer Property Log, identify and protect from damage or loss, notify customer of any loss/damage/unsuitability, return property as directed |

## 4. References

- QF-024 — Customer Property Log
- QP-008 — Nonconforming Product Control
- QP-023 — Customer Communication and Complaints
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 8.5.3 (Property Belonging to Customers or External Providers)

## 5. Definitions

- **Customer Property:** Any material, tooling, fixtures, intellectual property, data, or equipment provided by the customer for use in fulfilling a contract.
- **Customer Property Log:** Register tracking all customer-furnished items: receipt date, description, condition, storage location, and disposition/return date.

## 6. Procedure

### Receipt and Verification

1. Upon receipt of customer-furnished property, inspect for quantity and condition (damage, completeness).
2. Record in QF-024 Customer Property Log: date received, customer name, description, quantity, condition at receipt, associated PO/job number.
3. If property is received damaged or not as described, do NOT use. Notify the customer within 24 hours. Record the notification on QF-024.

### Identification and Tagging

4. Tag or label all customer property with "CUSTOMER PROPERTY" identification, customer name, and associated job number.
5. Do not intermingle customer property with shop-owned material or tooling without clear identification.

### Protection and Storage

6. Store customer property in designated areas appropriate to its type:
   - Material/castings: material rack, clearly labeled
   - Tooling/fixtures: tool crib or designated shelf, protected from damage
   - Drawings/data: controlled document area (physical or electronic)
7. Apply the same preservation practices as shop-owned items of similar type (corrosion prevention, climate control if required).
8. Do not use customer property for purposes other than the contracted work without written customer permission.

### Reporting Loss or Damage

9. If customer property is lost, damaged, or found unsuitable for use at any point:
   - Stop using the affected item immediately.
   - Record the event on QF-024 (date, description of loss/damage, circumstances).
   - Notify the customer within 24 hours per QP-023.
   - Retain damaged property for customer inspection unless otherwise directed.

### Return Process

10. Upon completion of the contracted work (or at customer request), return customer property in the condition received (normal wear excepted).
11. Record the return on QF-024: date returned, method of return, condition at return.
12. Obtain acknowledgment of return when feasible (shipping receipt, email confirmation).
