# QP-020 Handling, Storage, Packaging and Shipping

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the methods for handling, storing, packaging, and shipping product to prevent damage, contamination, deterioration, or loss from receipt of raw material through delivery to the customer.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all raw materials, in-process parts, finished parts, and customer property from the point of receipt through final shipment.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Ensure proper handling, maintain designated storage areas, select appropriate packaging methods, verify shipment contents against packing list, comply with customer-specified packaging requirements |

## 4. References

- QP-018 — FOD Prevention
- QP-021 — Customer Property Control
- QP-007 — Final Inspection and Product Release
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 8.5.4 (Preservation)

## 5. Definitions

- **VCI (Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor):** Paper or film that emits a corrosion-inhibiting vapor to protect metal parts in enclosed packaging.
- **Desiccant:** Moisture-absorbing material placed in sealed packaging to prevent humidity-related corrosion.
- **Preservation:** Activities to prevent deterioration of product quality during handling, storage, packaging, and transit.

## 6. Procedure

### Handling

1. Handle all parts with clean hands or clean gloves. Avoid touching precision-machined surfaces with bare skin (oils cause corrosion).
2. Use appropriate lifting techniques and equipment for heavy items to prevent dropping or impact damage.
3. Do not drag parts across surfaces. Lift and place.
4. Transport in-process parts between operations in protective containers or on designated clean racks.

### Storage

5. Store raw materials in the designated raw material area, organized by material type and clearly labeled with material specification and heat/lot number.
6. Store in-process parts in the designated WIP area, identified by Job Number.
7. Store finished parts awaiting shipment in the finished goods area, protected from shop contamination.
8. Materials with shelf life (adhesives, sealants, chemical treatments) shall be stored per manufacturer requirements and labeled with expiration dates. Do not use expired materials.
9. Periodically inspect stored materials for signs of corrosion, contamination, or damage.

### Packaging

10. Apply corrosion prevention (VCI paper/bags and/or desiccant packs) to all machined metal parts before sealing in packaging.
11. Wrap or separate individual parts to prevent contact damage during transit.
12. Use packaging materials appropriate to part size, weight, and fragility (foam, bubble wrap, dividers).
13. When customer-specified packaging requirements exist (noted on PO or drawing), follow those requirements exactly. Document compliance on the packing list.
14. For precision parts: double-box when shipping via common carrier to provide shock absorption.

### Shipping

15. Prepare shipping documentation package:
    - Packing list (part number, quantity, PO reference, drawing revision)
    - Certificate of Conformance (when required)
    - Material certifications (when required to be shipped with product)
    - Inspection reports (when contractually required)
16. Verify package contents against packing list before sealing.
17. Label outer packaging with: customer name, PO number, and "FRAGILE" or handling instructions as applicable.
18. Record shipment in the shipping log: date, customer, PO number, carrier, tracking number.
