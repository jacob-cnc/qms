# QP-023 Customer Communication

Standards and clause references are guidance only and do not claim certification or standard compliance. Applicable customer, contractual and legal requirements govern accepted work.

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for managing customer communications, handling customer complaints, and performing post-delivery support activities including warranty, returns, and field investigation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all customer communications related to quality, delivery, product conformity, complaints, and post-delivery activities for all contracts and orders.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Respond to customer inquiries, receive and document complaints, initiate corrective actions, provide notification of quality escapes or delivery issues, manage post-delivery activities (warranty returns, field investigations) |

## 4. References

- QF-022 — Customer Complaint Form
- QP-008 — Nonconforming Product Control
- QP-009 — Corrective Action
- QP-017 — Product Safety
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 8.2.1 (Customer Communication)
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 8.5.5 (Post-Delivery Activities)

## 5. Definitions

- **Customer Complaint:** Any expression of dissatisfaction from the customer regarding product quality, delivery, documentation, or service.
- **Notification Trigger:** An event requiring proactive communication to the customer before they discover the issue.
- **Post-Delivery Activity:** Any support, warranty, return, or investigation activity occurring after product has been delivered to the customer.

## 6. Procedure

### Communication Methods and Documentation

1. Customer communications regarding quality or contractual matters shall be documented. Acceptable methods: email (retained), phone (followed up with email summary), or formal letter.
2. Retain records of quality-related communications in the job file or customer folder for the duration of the record retention period.

### Complaint Handling

3. Upon receipt of a customer complaint:
   - Acknowledge receipt to the customer within 24 hours.
   - Initiate QF-022 Customer Complaint Record: date received, customer, description of complaint, affected part/PO/job numbers.
4. Investigate the complaint: determine root cause, scope (how many parts affected), and whether corrective action is required.
5. Provide the customer with a formal response including: findings, corrective/preventive actions taken, and timeline for resolution.
6. If corrective action is warranted, initiate per QP-009.
7. Close QF-022 when the customer accepts the response and corrective actions are verified effective.

### Notification Triggers (Proactive)

8. Notify the customer proactively when ANY of the following occur:
   - Nonconforming product has been shipped (quality escape) — notify within 24 hours of discovery.
   - Safety-critical nonconformance discovered (per QP-017) — notify within 24 hours.
   - Delivery will not meet the committed date — notify as soon as the delay is known.
   - Process or source change that may require customer approval.
9. Record all proactive notifications: date, method, person contacted, information provided, customer response.

### Record Retention

10. Retain customer complaint records (QF-022) and associated corrective actions under QP-001 and SUP-003 for at least ten years after the relevant last shipment or record completion, applying the longest customer, contract, program or legal requirement.

## 5. Post-Delivery Activities

### Warranty and Returns

11. When a customer reports a quality issue after delivery (warranty claim or return):
    - Issue a Return Material Authorization (RMA) number.
    - Upon receipt of returned product, inspect and document findings on QF-022.
    - Disposition per QP-008 (rework, replace, or credit as appropriate).
12. Retain records of returned product disposition for the contract retention period.

### Field Investigation Support

13. When a customer requests investigation support for product in service:
    - Provide technical data, manufacturing records, and inspection records as requested.
    - Support failure analysis by providing process history, material certifications, and dimensional records.
14. Document all field investigation support provided, including data released to the customer.

### Record Retention After Delivery

15. Retain manufacturing records (inspection data, material certs, process records, FAI) for the greater of:
    - 10 years after the relevant last delivery, OR
    - The period specified by customer contract/purchase order, OR
    - As required by applicable regulations
16. Records subject to retention include: inspection reports, material certifications, NCRs/CARs, FAI packages, and traceability data.
