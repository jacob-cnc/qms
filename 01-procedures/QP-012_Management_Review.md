# QP-012 Management Review

## 1. Purpose

Define the process for conducting periodic, structured reviews of the QMS to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, and alignment with business objectives.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the entire Quality Management System. Uses a structured owner self-review of actual performance and applicable customer requirements. AS9100 clause references are guidance only; no certification or standard compliance is claimed.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Shop Owner | Schedule reviews, gather input data, conduct the structured self-review, document decisions and actions, track action items to closure |

## 4. References

- QF-015 - Management Review Minutes
- QF-038 - Improvement Log
- QM-003 - Quality Objectives
- QP-011 - Internal Audit
- QP-009 - Corrective Action
- AS9100 Rev D Clause 9.3

## 5. Definitions

- **Management Review:** A formal, planned evaluation of the QMS by top management (Shop Owner) to assess performance, identify improvements, and make resource/strategic decisions.
- **Owner Self-Review:** A structured, data-driven review process conducted by the Shop Owner serving as both management and worker, using documented evidence and predetermined agenda items to ensure objectivity.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Frequency and Scheduling

1. Conduct management review at least every quarter in which customer work is performed, and at least annually to review readiness and continuing suitability when no customer work is performed.
2. Include the latest annual owner self-check and open actions when available; do not delay quarterly reviews while waiting for a self-check.
3. Ad-hoc reviews may be conducted when significant changes occur (new customer requirements, major nonconformance trends, business changes).

### 6.2 Required Inputs (Prepare Before Review)

4. Gather relevant available data before each review. The following agenda uses AS9100 clause 9.3.2 as guidance; mark unexercised or inapplicable activities accordingly:
   a. Status of actions from previous management reviews (9.3.2 a)
   b. Changes in external and internal issues relevant to the QMS (9.3.2 b)
   c. Information on QMS performance and effectiveness including (9.3.2 c):
      - Customer satisfaction and feedback (9.3.2 c.1)
      - Extent to which quality objectives have been met (9.3.2 c.2)
      - Process performance and conformity of products and services (9.3.2 c.3)
      - Nonconformities and corrective actions (9.3.2 c.4)
      - Monitoring and measurement results (9.3.2 c.5)
      - Audit results (9.3.2 c.6)
      - Performance of external providers / suppliers (9.3.2 c.7)
   d. Adequacy of resources (9.3.2 d)
   e. Effectiveness of actions taken to address risks and opportunities (9.3.2 e)
   f. Opportunities for improvement (9.3.2 f)
   g. On-time delivery performance (AS9100-specific)
   h. Feedback from relevant interested parties (AS9100-specific)

### 6.3 Conducting the Review (Self-Review Method)

5. Set aside enough time for a useful review; brief minutes are sufficient when they capture the evidence, decisions and actions.
6. Work through each input systematically using QF-015 Management Review Minutes template as the agenda.
7. For each input area, document:
   - Current status/data (quantitative where possible)
   - Trend compared to previous review (improving, stable, declining)
   - Actions needed (if any)
8. Be honest and self-critical. The value of the review depends on objective assessment, not optimistic reporting.

### 6.4 Required Outputs (Decisions and Actions)

9. Document decisions and actions for each of the following:
   a. Opportunities for improvement and specific actions planned
   b. Any need for changes to the QMS (procedures, processes, resources)
   c. Resource needs (equipment, training, contracted services)
   d. Updated quality objectives if current ones are met or no longer relevant
10. Assign target dates and responsible person (Shop Owner) for each action item.

### 6.5 Action Tracking

11. Transfer action items to QF-038 Improvement Log or to individual CARs as appropriate.
12. Review status of all open management review actions at the next review (Input 4a).
13. Actions not completed by target date require documented justification and revised target.

### 6.6 Records

14. Complete and sign QF-015 Management Review Minutes.
15. Retain management review records under QP-001 and SUP-003 for at least ten years after completion, or longer when another applicable requirement governs.
