# QM-003 Quality Objectives

## 1. Purpose

Define measurable quality objectives that support the Quality Policy (QM-002), provide a basis for monitoring QMS performance, and drive continual improvement. These objectives are reviewed at each Management Review (QP-012) for continuing suitability.

## 2. Scope

These objectives apply to all QMS processes and are tracked by the Shop Owner using QF-037 Quality Objectives Tracker.

## 3. Quality Objectives

| # | Objective | KPI | Target | Measurement Method | Frequency | Responsible |
|---|-----------|-----|--------|-------------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | On-Time Delivery | Percentage of orders delivered by customer-requested date | >= 95% | (Orders delivered on time / Total orders shipped) x 100 | Monthly | Shop Owner |
| 2 | First-Pass Yield | Percentage of parts passing final inspection without rework or scrap | >= 98% | (Parts accepted at final inspection / Total parts inspected) x 100 | Monthly | Shop Owner |
| 3 | Customer Complaints | Number of customer complaints or returns received | < 2 per quarter | Count of QF-022 Customer Complaint forms initiated | Quarterly | Shop Owner |
| 4 | Corrective Action Closure | Average time to close corrective action requests | <= 30 calendar days | Average days from CAR initiation (QF-012) to verified closure | Quarterly | Shop Owner |
| 5 | Calibration Compliance | Percentage of measurement equipment calibrated on schedule | 100% | (Instruments calibrated on time / Total instruments due) x 100 | Monthly | Shop Owner |
| 6 | Supplier Quality | Percentage of incoming material/services accepted at receiving inspection | >= 98% | (Lots accepted / Total lots received) x 100 | Quarterly | Shop Owner |

## 4. Objective Details

### 4.1 On-Time Delivery (OTD)

- **Why it matters:** Late delivery directly impacts customer satisfaction and may result in loss of future orders
- **Data source:** Job Traveler (QF-004) ship date vs. customer-requested delivery date
- **Escalation:** If OTD drops below 90% in any month, initiate root cause analysis and consider corrective action (QP-009)

### 4.2 First-Pass Yield (FPY)

- **Why it matters:** Rework and scrap consume capacity in a single-operator shop and indicate process control issues
- **Data source:** Final Inspection Reports (QF-006) - count of accept vs. reject dispositions
- **Escalation:** If FPY drops below 95% in any month, initiate corrective action (QP-009)

### 4.3 Customer Complaints

- **Why it matters:** Complaints indicate escaped nonconformances or failures to meet expectations
- **Data source:** Customer Complaint forms (QF-022) logged in NCR/CAR Log
- **Escalation:** Any complaint involving product safety triggers immediate corrective action (QP-009)

### 4.4 Corrective Action Closure Time

- **Why it matters:** Open CARs represent unresolved systemic issues; timely closure demonstrates effective corrective action
- **Data source:** NCR/CAR Log - date opened vs. date closed with effectiveness verification
- **Escalation:** Any CAR open beyond 60 days is flagged for Management Review

### 4.5 Calibration Compliance

- **Why it matters:** Out-of-calibration equipment may produce nonconforming product; 100% compliance is an AS9100 expectation
- **Data source:** Calibration Log (QF-020) and Calibration Schedule (QF-030)
- **Escalation:** Any missed calibration triggers Out-of-Tolerance Impact Assessment (QF-033) per QP-010

### 4.6 Supplier Quality Acceptance

- **Why it matters:** Incoming nonconformances consume inspection time and may escape to production
- **Data source:** Receiving Inspection Records (QF-003) - accept vs. reject per lot
- **Escalation:** If a supplier's acceptance rate drops below 95%, initiate supplier corrective action (QF-035)

## 5. Tracking and Review

### 5.1 Data Collection

The Shop Owner records objective measurements using QF-037 Quality Objectives Tracker. Data is collected per the frequency specified in Section 3.

### 5.2 Management Review Input

Quality objective performance is a mandatory input to Management Review (QP-012). The review evaluates:
- Whether targets are being met
- Trends (improving, stable, or declining)
- Whether objectives remain relevant and appropriately challenging
- Need for corrective action or objective revision

### 5.3 Objective Revision

Objectives may be revised during Management Review when:
- Targets have been consistently met for 4+ consecutive periods (increase challenge)
- Targets are proven unrealistic based on process capability data
- Business context changes (new equipment, new customer requirements, scope change)

All revisions to this document follow QP-001 Document Control and QP-025 Control of Changes.
