# NCR/CAR Log

## Instructions

This register tracks all Nonconformance Reports (NCRs) and Corrective Action Reports (CARs) issued. Maintain this log as a running record. Number sequentially starting from NCR-001 and CAR-001. When an NCR requires systemic corrective action, escalate to a CAR and cross-reference.

**Numbering Convention:**
- NCRs: NCR-001, NCR-002, NCR-003...
- CARs: CAR-001, CAR-002, CAR-003...

**When to Escalate NCR to CAR:**
- Repeat nonconformity (same issue occurs twice)
- Customer escape (nonconforming product shipped)
- Systemic issue identified (process/procedure failure vs. one-time error)
- Customer complaint received
- Internal audit finding requiring corrective action
