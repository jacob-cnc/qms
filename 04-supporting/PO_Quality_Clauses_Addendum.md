## Purpose

This addendum is attached to all purchase orders issued by [Company Name]. By accepting the purchase order, the supplier agrees to comply with all clauses listed below. These clauses apply to the supplier and shall be flowed down to all sub-tier suppliers providing product or services related to the order.

## Quality Clauses

### Clause 1: Compliance with Specifications
Supplier shall comply with all specifications, drawings, standards, and other technical requirements referenced on or attached to the purchase order. The latest revision applies unless a specific revision is stated. Supplier shall not make substitutions without prior written approval.

### Clause 2: Right of Access
Right of access is granted to [Company Name], its customers, and regulatory authorities (including FAA, EASA, DCMA, and customer representatives) to all facilities, records, and personnel involved in the order. This includes the supplier's premises and any sub-tier supplier locations.

### Clause 3: Notification of Nonconformity
Supplier shall notify [Company Name] within 24 hours of discovery of any nonconforming product, material, or process that may affect items on order. Supplier shall not ship nonconforming product without prior written disposition from [Company Name].

### Clause 4: Record Retention
Supplier shall retain all quality records related to this order for a minimum of 10 years or life-of-program, whichever is longer. Records include but are not limited to: inspection reports, material certifications, test results, traceability records, and process records.

### Clause 5: Counterfeit Parts Prevention
Supplier shall maintain controls to prevent the use and introduction of counterfeit parts and materials in accordance with AS6174 and AS6081. Supplier shall only source from OCMs, authorized distributors, or sources approved by [Company Name]. Suspect or confirmed counterfeit parts shall be quarantined and reported to [Company Name] within 24 hours.

### Clause 6: Flow-Down to Sub-Tier Suppliers
All applicable requirements of this purchase order, including these quality clauses, applicable customer requirements, and regulatory requirements, shall be flowed down to sub-tier suppliers. Supplier retains full responsibility for sub-tier compliance.

### Clause 7: Change Notification
Supplier shall notify [Company Name] in writing prior to any changes to product, process, source of supply, manufacturing location, or special process approvals. No changes shall be implemented without written approval. This includes changes to sub-tier suppliers.

### Clause 8: Certificate of Conformance (C of C)
A Certificate of Conformance is required with each shipment. The C of C shall include: part number, revision, purchase order number, quantity, statement of conformance to all PO requirements, and authorized signature with date.

### Clause 9: Material Certifications and Traceability
Material certifications (mill test reports) with heat number and/or lot traceability are required for all raw materials and shall accompany each shipment. Certifications must be traceable to the specific material supplied. Chemical and physical properties must meet the applicable material specification.

### Clause 10: DFARS Compliance
Where applicable, supplier shall comply with DFARS 252.225-7009 (Restriction on Acquisition of Certain Articles Containing Specialty Metals). Country of melt and pour for specialty metals shall be documented and provided upon request. Supplier shall maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance.

## Applicability

These clauses apply to all purchase orders unless specifically excluded in writing on the individual order. Additional clauses may be specified on the purchase order as required by specific customer contracts or regulatory requirements.

## Supplier Acknowledgment

Acceptance of the purchase order constitutes acceptance of all quality clauses contained herein. Questions regarding these requirements should be directed to [Company Name] Quality prior to acceptance.
