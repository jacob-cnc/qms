# SUP-003 Record Retention Schedule

## 1. Rule and Scope

Retain all quality records for at least **ten years** after the relevant last shipment. For records not associated with a shipment, start at record completion. Apply any longer customer, contract, program, purchase-order or legal requirement; the longest applicable period always governs. Standards references are guidance and do not claim certification or standard compliance.

## 2. Record Groups

| Record Group | Minimum / Additional Rule |
|---|---|
| Contract reviews, POs, communications and customer approvals | Ten-year rule above; apply longer order/program/legal requirements |
| Receiving, material/process certifications, travelers, traceability, inspection, CoCs and formal FAI when required | Ten-year rule above; apply longer order/program/legal requirements |
| Nonconformances, complaints, corrective actions and counterfeit investigations | Ten-year rule above; apply longer order/program/legal requirements |
| Owner self-checks, management reviews, risk assessments and superseded versions, objectives, improvements, FOD and training/competence records | Ten-year rule above; apply longer applicable requirements |
| Supplier approval, evaluation and performance records | At least ten years after the last related delivery or record completion, whichever is later, and longer when required |
| Calibration and maintenance records | Equipment life plus three years after retirement, or the ten-year rule above or another applicable requirement, whichever is longest |
| Customer-property records | At least ten years after the relevant last shipment or property return, whichever is later, and longer when required |
| Document-change and superseded-document records | At least ten years after supersession/record completion or the last related shipment, whichever is later, and longer when required |
| CNC programs and drawing records used for production | Retain the applicable versions with the job history under the ten-year rule; do not erase required superseded versions |

“Life of program” applies when required by the order/program. Confirm its meaning and retention end point with the customer; do not substitute an arbitrary default end date when it is unknown.

## 3. Storage, Backup and Access

The owner keeps legible, identifiable records by job, customer, instrument or equipment ID, as applicable. Electronic records are stored on the owner's administrative computer at the single shop facility; physical records, where used, are protected in appropriate folders/binders.

Back up electronic QMS, job and CNC program records to an external hard drive **weekly and whenever documents or records are added or changed**. Test retrieval at least annually and record completion on REG-005 QMS Recurring Tasks. Keep the originals and backup protected against unauthorized access, loss and damage. Access to customer data follows customer restrictions and QM-001.

Provide records to the customer or authorities when required by the applicable contract, protecting other customers' information. Do not destroy records while any applicable retention period, open issue or preservation obligation remains. Dispose securely only after the owner verifies eligibility and any required customer authorization.
