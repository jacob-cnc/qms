## Purpose

This document defines the minimum retention periods for all quality records generated by the QMS. It ensures compliance with AS9100 Rev D clause 7.5, customer contractual requirements, and applicable regulatory requirements.

## Storage Methods

**Physical Records:** Filed in labeled binders or folders organized by record type and year. Stored in the shop office area protected from environmental damage (moisture, heat, FOD).

**Electronic Records:** Stored on the shop computer with regular backup. File naming convention: [Record Type]_[Job/ID Number]_[Date YYYY-MM-DD]. Backup minimum monthly to external drive or cloud storage.

## Important Notes

1. **Customer requirements take precedence** - If a customer contract specifies a longer retention period, that period applies. Check contract review (QF-001) for specific retention requirements.

2. **Life of program** - Means as long as the part/assembly is in active service. If unknown, default to 20 years from last delivery.

3. **Destruction method** - Physical records: shredding. Electronic records: permanent deletion from all storage locations including backups.

4. **Retention start date** - Begins from date of last delivery for production records, or from record completion date for administrative records.

5. **Audit access** - Records within retention period must be retrievable within 24 hours of request.

## Annual Review

This schedule shall be reviewed during management review (QP-012) to ensure it remains current with customer and regulatory requirements.
