## 🔧 One-Time Setup (7 docs)
**Fill out ONCE during QMS implementation.** Review and update annually at Management Review, but the initial creation is the heavy lift.

| Doc ID | Title | What to fill in |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| QF-019 | Approved Supplier List | Your current material, calibration, and special process suppliers |
| QF-027 | Equipment List | Your 3 machines + supporting equipment |
| QF-028 | Measurement Equipment List | Every gage, micrometer, caliper, indicator — with serial #s and cal dates |
| QF-030 | Calibration Schedule | Due dates for each instrument based on your chosen intervals |
| QF-032 | Context of the Organization | Internal/external issues, interested parties (already partially in QM-001) |
| QF-036 | Training Matrix | Competencies required vs. your current qualifications |
| QF-037 | Quality Objectives Tracker | Your measurable targets (OTD, yield, etc.) and how you'll track them |

## 🔁 Per-Job (10 docs)
**Fill out for EVERY job/purchase order.** These are your production quality records — the paper trail an auditor will sample.

| Doc ID | Title | When used |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| QF-001 | Contract Review Checklist | When you accept a new PO |
| QF-002 | Purchase Order | When you buy material or outside services for a job |
| QF-003 | Receiving Inspection Record | When material/parts arrive |
| QF-004 | Job Traveler / Route Sheet | Accompanies every part through the shop (THE core production record) |
| QF-005 | In-Process Inspection Record | At each machining operation |
| QF-006 | Final Inspection Report | Before releasing parts for shipment |
| QF-007 | FAI Form 1 — Part Number Accountability | First time making a new part (or after trigger events) |
| QF-008 | FAI Form 2 — Product Accountability | (same — material/process traceability) |
| QF-009 | FAI Form 3 — Characteristic Accountability | (same — every dimension measured) |
| QF-010 | Certificate of Conformance | Ships with every delivery to customer |

**Note:** QF-007/008/009 (FAI) are only required when FAI is triggered — not every single job. But for your first jobs, everything is a first article.

## ⚡ Event-Triggered (10 docs)
**Fill out when something HAPPENS.** You may never use some of these if the triggering event doesn't occur — but they must exist and you must know when to pull them.

| Doc ID | Title | Trigger |
|--------|-------|---------|
| QF-011 | Nonconforming Material Report (NCR) | Part fails inspection, wrong material received, customer return |
| QF-012 | Corrective Action Report (8D) | Repeat NC, NC escapes to customer, safety issue, significant scrap |
| QF-022 | Customer Complaint Form | Customer reports a problem |
| QF-024 | Customer Property Log | Customer sends you material, tooling, or drawings to safeguard |
| QF-025 | Document Change Request | You need to revise a QMS procedure or form |
| QF-029 | Change Notification Form | Production process change that could affect product |
| QF-031 | Suspect Counterfeit Material Report | Material looks wrong, certs don't match, suspicious source |
| QF-033 | Out-of-Tolerance Impact Assessment | Gage found OOT at calibration |
| QF-034 | Supplier Audit Checklist | You audit a supplier (or evaluate a new critical one) |
| QF-035 | Supplier Corrective Action Request | Supplier delivers NC material or misses delivery |

## Implementation Priority

**Start here (Week 1):**
1. One-time setup forms (QF-019, QF-027, QF-028, QF-030, QF-032, QF-036, QF-037)
2. Daily FOD checklist habit (QF-026)

**Then run your first job through (Week 2+):**
3. QF-001 → QF-002 → QF-003 → QF-004 → QF-005 → QF-006 → QF-010

**Build evidence over 3-6 months, then:**
4. Management Review (QF-015)
5. Internal Audit (QF-013, QF-014)
6. Schedule Stage 1 with registrar
